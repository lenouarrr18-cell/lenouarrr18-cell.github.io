Privacy policy

Dernière mise à jour : 20 novembre 2026

In short

Club Owner can be played without an account, ads or analytics. If you choose to sign in with Google to back up your career online, only the data needed for that service is used (see below).

Google account and cloud save (optional)

If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address, name and an account identifier from Google. Your career save (club, players, finances, achievements) is then stored on Google Firebase servers (Google LLC) so you can restore it on another device. This data is never sold, shared or used for advertising. You can sign out at any time and request deletion of your online save at dekigiannisemmanuels@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

To work, the Game stores locally: your progress (club, squad, finances, history), your settings (language, difficulty), the names you type and your acceptance of the terms. This data stays on your device and is never sent to the publisher or third parties.

Permissions

The Game asks for no sensitive permission (location, contacts, camera, microphone, files).

Retention and deletion

You can delete your data at any time: Settings > New game, clearing the app data in your phone settings, or uninstalling.

Children

As the Game collects no data, it can be used by everyone. Parents remain responsible for their children's use.

Your rights

Under Ivorian law no. 2013-450 on personal data protection and, where applicable, the EU GDPR, you have rights of access, rectification and erasure. As the publisher holds no data about you, these rights are exercised directly on your device. Contact: dekigiannisemmanuels@gmail.com.

Changes

If online services (cloud saves, leaderboards, purchases) are added, this policy will be updated and your consent requested before any collection.
